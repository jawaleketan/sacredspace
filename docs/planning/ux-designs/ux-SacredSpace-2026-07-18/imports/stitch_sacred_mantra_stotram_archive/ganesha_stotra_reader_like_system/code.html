Ganesha Stotra

The Auspicious Hymn to Lord Ganesha

Lord Ganesha Protection Success

The Ganesha Stotra, attributed to Sage Narada, is a powerful prayer for the removal of all obstacles. Recited daily, it is said to bring wisdom, wealth, and spiritual liberation to the seeker.

01

प्रणम्य शिरसा देवं गौरीपुत्रं विनायकम् ।
भक्तावासं स्मरेन्नित्यमायुष्कामार्थसिद्धये ॥ १ ॥

praṇamya śirasā devaṃ gaurīputraṃ vināyakam ।
bhaktāvāsaṃ smarennityamāyuṣkāmārthasiddhaye ॥ 1 ॥

Bowing with his head, one should daily remember the Lord, the son of Gauri, Vinayaka, who lives in the hearts of his devotees, for the attainment of long life, desires, and wealth.

02

प्रथमं वक्रतुण्डं च एकदन्तं द्वितीयकम् ।
तृतीयं कृष्णपिङ्गाक्षं गजवक्त्रं चतुर्थकम् ॥ २ ॥

prathamaṃ vakratuṇḍaṃ ca ekadantaṃ dvitīyakam ।
tṛtīyaṃ kṛṣṇapiṅgākṣaṃ gajavaktraṃ caturthakam ॥ 2 ॥

First, the one with the twisted trunk; second, the one with a single tusk; third, the one with dark brown eyes; and fourth, the one with an elephant's head.

03

लम्बोदरं पञ्चमं च षष्ठं विकटमेव च ।
सप्तमं विघ्नराजं च धूम्रवर्णं तथाष्टमम् ॥ ३ ॥

lambodaraṃ pañcamaṃ ca ṣaṣṭhaṃ vikaṭameva ca ।
saptamaṃ vighnarājaṃ ca dhūmravarṇaṃ tathāṣṭamam ॥ 3 ॥

Fifth, the one with a large belly; sixth, the one who is formidable; seventh, the king of obstacles; and eighth, the one with a smoke-colored complexion.

04

नवमं भालचन्द्रं च दशमं तु विनायकम् ।
एकादशं गणपतिं द्वादशं तु गजाननम् ॥ ४ ॥

navamaṃ bhālacandraṃ ca daśamaṃ tu vināyakam ।
ekādaśaṃ gaṇapatiṃ daśamaṃ tu gajānanam ॥ 4 ॥

Ninth, the one with the moon on his forehead; tenth, the leader of obstacles; eleventh, the lord of the Ganas; and twelfth, the one with the elephant face.

ॐ गम गणपतये नमः

Om Gam Ganapataye Namaha
