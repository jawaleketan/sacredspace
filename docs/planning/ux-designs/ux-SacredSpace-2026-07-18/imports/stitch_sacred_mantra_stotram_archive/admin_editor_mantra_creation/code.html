Success Protection Narada
Devanagari Script
प्रणम्य शिरसा देवं गौरीपुत्रं विनायकम् ।
भक्तावासं स्मरेन्नित्यमायुष्कामार्थसिद्धये ॥ १ ॥
Transliteration
praṇamya śirasā devaṃ gaurīputraṃ vināyakam |
bhaktāvāsaṃ smarennityamāyuṣkāmārthasiddhaye || 1 ||
Meaning
Bowing my head with reverence to the Divine Lord Ganesha, the son of Goddess Gauri, I meditate daily on the abode of devotees for the fulfillment of longevity, desires, and spiritual purposes.
