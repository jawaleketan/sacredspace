"The Five Jewels of Ganesha"
01

मुदाकरात्तमोदकं सदा विमुक्तिसाधकं
कलाधरावतंसकं विलासिलोक रक्षकम् ।
अनायकैकनायकं विनाशितेभदैत्यकं
नताशुभाशुनाशकं नमामि तं विनायकम् ॥ १ ॥

Mudākarātta Modakaṃ Sadā Vimukti Sādhakaṃ
Kalādharāvataṃsakaṃ Vilāsiloka Rakshakam |
Anāyakaika Nāyakaṃ Vināśitebha Daityakaṃ
Natāśubhāśu Nāśakaṃ Namāmi Taṃ Vināyakam || 1 ||

I salute Ganesha, the lord who holds the modaka in his hand with joy, who always bestows liberation, who wears the crescent moon on his head, who protects this world of appearances, who is the sole leader of those who have no leader, who destroyed the elephant demon, and who quickly destroys the obstacles of those who bow down.

02

नतेतरातिभीकरं नवोदितार्कभास्वरं
नमत्सुरारिनिर्जरं नताधिकापदुद्धरम् ।
सुरेश्वरं निधीश्वरं गजेश्वरं गणेश्वरं
महेश्वरं तमाश्रये परात्परं निरन्तरम् ॥ २ ॥

Natetarātibhīkaraṃ Navoditārkabhāsvaraṃ
Namatsurārinirjaraṃ Natādhikāpaduddharam |
Sureśvaraṃ Nidhīśvaraṃ Gajeśvaraṃ Gaṇeśvaraṃ
Maheśvaraṃ Tamāśraye Parātparaṃ Nirantaram || 2 ||

I seek shelter in that Supreme Lord Ganesha, who is terrible to those who do not bow, who shines like the newly risen sun, who is saluted by both gods and demons, who rescues his devotees from great calamities, the lord of gods, the lord of treasures, the lord of elephants, the lord of Ganas, and the great lord of all.

Word Analysis

मुदा (Muda)

With Joy

Derived from the root 'mud', meaning happiness or delight.

विमुक्ति (Vimukti)

Liberation

Total freedom from the cycle of birth and death (Samsara).

रक्षकम् (Rakshakam)

Protector

One who guards or preserves his devotees from harm.

विनायकम् (Vinayakam)

Remover of Obstacles

The supreme leader who has no leader above him.

सुरेश्वरं (Sureshvaram)

Lord of Gods

From 'Sura' (gods) and 'Ishvara' (lord).

निरन्तरम् (Nirantaram)

Eternal

Without interruption, continuous, or everlasting.
