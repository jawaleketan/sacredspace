Ganesha Puja: Invoking the Remover of Obstacles

A foundational ritual for clarity, wisdom, and successful beginnings, honoring the elephant-headed deity of thresholds.

Deity Archetype

Lord Ganesha

inventory_2 Essential Ingredients

Small Ganesha Idol or Picture
Incense sticks (Agarbatti)
Fresh Flowers (Red or Orange)
Copper or Brass bowl of Water
Sandalwood paste or Turmeric
Modaks or fresh Fruits (Prasad)
"Arrange these items on a clean cloth-covered surface in a quiet corner of your home before beginning."
01

Purification & Setting Intent

Begin by sitting in a comfortable cross-legged position facing North or East. Sprinkle a few drops of water over yourself and your immediate surroundings to symbolically purify the space. Close your eyes and visualize Ganesha, breathing deeply to center your awareness.

Chant this Mantra

ॐ अपवित्रः पवित्रो वा सर्वावस्थां गतोऽपि वा ।
यः स्मरेत्पुण्डरीकाक्षं स बाह्याभ्यन्तरः शुचिः ॥

"Om Apavitrah Pavitro Va... One who remembers the lotus-eyed Lord becomes pure inside and out."

02

The Invocation (Avahana)

Place the idol gently on the pedestal. Light the incense and offer the flowers one by one at the feet of the deity. As you do so, mentally invite the divine presence into your space, asking for the removal of obstacles in your current path.

Invocation Mantra

ॐ गं गणपतये नमः ॥

"Om Gam Ganapataye Namaha. Salutations to the Giver of Wisdom."

03

Offering Light & Sustenance

Rotate the lit lamp (Deepam) or incense three times in a clockwise direction in front of the idol. Offer the sweets or fruits (Prasad) with a sincere heart. Spend 10 minutes in silent meditation, absorbing the energy of the ritual.

stars

Ritual Complete

Take a moment to bow. Your space is now energized with the presence of Ganesha. May your path be clear.
