Gayatri Mantra

The universal prayer for enlightenment, seeking the divine light to guide our intellect.

I
ॐ भूर्भुवः स्वः ।
Oṃ bhūr bhuvaḥ svaḥ
Invocation of the three planes of existence: physical, astral, and celestial.
तत्सवितुर्वरेण्यं ।
Tat savitur vareṇyaṃ
Meditating upon the adorable glory of the radiant solar deity.
भर्गो देवस्य धीमहि ।
Bhargo devasya dhīmahi
May that divine light illuminate our meditation.
धियो यो नः प्रचोदयात् ॥
Dhiyo yo naḥ pracodayāt
Inspire our intellect and guide us toward awakening.

menu_book Word Analysis

Savituḥ

Derived from "Savitṛ", referring to the Sun as the progenitor and stimulator of life.

Vareṇyaṃ

"The most excellent" or "worthy of being adored," from the root "vṛ" (to choose).

Bhargaḥ

Radiance, luster, or the spiritual light that destroys ignorance.

Dhīmahi

An ancient optative verb form meaning "we meditate" or "may we absorb."
